My research is on intelligent systems and applied machine learning — in particular, building models that hold up outside the lab: learning from noisy, real-world signals and turning them into systems that can actually be deployed.

Alongside research, I enjoy building and shipping small, practical tools.

I am always glad to hear from people working on related problems, so feel free to get in touch about collaborations, ideas, or just to talk research.
